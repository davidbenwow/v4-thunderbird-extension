How it works:

  • The V4 toolbar icon is gray when the open message has nothing to mark, and shows the orange ring when it contains a lead that needs action.
  • Click the icon to see the lead's current status, then use Mark as response or Mark as manuscript received to open the lead in V4 and set the status there. The lead stays "to mark" until its V4 status actually changes.
  • The extension also scans the quoted thread — useful when a prospect replies from a different address.
  • You need to be logged into v4.vdm-vsg.de in your browser for the Mark button to work.
